What does Google review removal cost at ReviewsBoost?

ReviewsBoost charges CAD $45 upfront per accepted review and CAD $95 only if that review is removed. Start with a free assessment. You’ll see the scope and fees before deciding to approve the work.

ReviewsBoost fees and example totals

ReviewsBoost bills per accepted review. Your written scope lists the reviews included, the work our team will handle and the terms for each success fee.

Once you approve a paid order, the upfront fee is CAD $45 per review in the order. The CAD $95 success fee applies to each review that meets the removal criteria in your written agreement. A review that stays online incurs only the upfront fee, subject to the cancellation and refund terms.

What the ReviewsBoost upfront fee covers

After you approve paid work, we prepare the case and handle the reporting and follow-up in your written scope. Depending on the case, that work can include:

  • reviewing the review text, rating, profile and surrounding facts;
  • matching the concern to the most relevant current policy category;
  • organizing screenshots, URLs, dates and supporting evidence;
  • managing the initial submission and recording its status;
  • preparing a concise appeal where Google makes that option available;
  • providing status updates and confirming whether the review remains visible.

Can you pay to remove Google reviews?

Yes. With ReviewsBoost, you can pay a specialist to assess a Google review, prepare evidence, manage the report and help with an eligible appeal. If you’ve already reported the review, share the latest status so we can review the next step.

If you have several suspicious reviews, an earlier report was declined, or you’re unsure which evidence matters, it may be worth having a specialist look at the case. At ReviewsBoost, the first assessment is free. You pay the upfront fee only after approving the work and the success fee only for reviews that meet the agreed removal terms.

Which reviews does ReviewsBoost assess?

We assess reviews with possible policy issues such as fake engagement, a conflict of interest, harassment or exposed personal information. A short note about what happened helps tie your concern to the specific review. See the Google review policy guide for examples.

When ReviewsBoost charges the success fee

The CAD $95 success fee applies individually to each review that meets your written removal terms. If you approve work on five reviews and three are removed, you pay three success fees. Use the fee calculator to compare totals for your review count.

What updates come with a ReviewsBoost case?

ReviewsBoost confirms the proposed next steps before you approve. While we handle your case, we share progress, requests for additional information and the outcome for each review.

Questions about ReviewsBoost fees

Is the ReviewsBoost assessment free?

Yes. Send the reviews you want assessed. We’ll explain the proposed work and fees before you decide to go ahead.

What is the maximum ReviewsBoost fee for one removed Google review?

Under the published $45 upfront plus $95 after-removal model, the service fee totals $140 CAD for one review that is successfully removed. Applicable taxes should be confirmed in the written checkout or service agreement.

When is the $95 success fee charged?

It applies only to a review that meets the agreed removal terms. See the fee and outcome terms.

Can ReviewsBoost assess several reviews at once?

Several reviews can be assessed and tracked in the same service period, but each should have its own URL, evidence and outcome record. ReviewsBoost calculates the stated upfront and success fees per review.

Where can I request an exact total?

Choose the number of reviews in the Google review removal request section. The form collects the public business and review details needed for an initial assessment and does not take payment when submitted.
