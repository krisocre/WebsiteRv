Refund and cancellation information

Use this process to request review of a cancellation, duplicate charge, delivery issue or refund question.

Effective September 2, 2026
Order-specific information: the written quote, invoice, checkout page or service agreement accepted for an order contains the applicable scope and cancellation details. Keep a copy with the transaction record.

Sending an enquiry does not take payment

The website forms send your request for assessment. They do not collect payment-card details or charge the initial fee. We discuss eligibility, scope and written terms before you decide to order.

Which terms apply?

The written quote, invoice, checkout page or service agreement accepted for an order should state the price, currency, scope, start point, cancellation terms, delivery conditions and any guarantee. Those order-specific terms are the first reference for a refund request, subject to rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

Requesting cancellation

Email support@reviewsboost.ca as soon as possible with “Cancellation request” in the subject. Work may begin after an order is accepted, so prompt contact matters. Include:

  • customer and business name;
  • email used for the order;
  • order, invoice or transaction reference;
  • service and transaction date;
  • reason for the request; and
  • the requested resolution.

Duplicate or unauthorized charges

Report a suspected duplicate or unauthorized charge promptly. Do not send full payment-card details by email. Provide only the transaction date, amount, receipt or invoice reference and the last four digits if needed for identification. Secure identity verification may be required.

Service concerns

If the concern involves delivery, describe the promised scope and the part believed to be incomplete. Screenshots and dated correspondence can help. For third-party platform outcomes, remember that ReviewsBoost does not control Google, Meta, Yelp, Tripadvisor, Trustpilot, Glassdoor or any platform’s decisions, availability or timing; the accepted service terms should explain what work is promised independently of the outcome.

Review-removal fees

Every public removal page describes a CAD $30 initial fee per accepted review. The per-review fee charged after successful removal varies by platform and is displayed before submission: Google $40, Facebook $45, Yelp $55, Tripadvisor $65, Trustpilot $75 and Glassdoor $95 CAD. Before ordering, request written confirmation of what the upfront fee covers, what counts as successful removal, when the later fee becomes due, what happens if content returns, and which cases qualify for any advertised guarantee.

Processing

After eligibility is confirmed, a refund should be returned through the original payment method when reasonably possible. Banks and payment providers control posting times. ReviewsBoost should provide written confirmation of the decision and amount.

Questions or unresolved concerns

Contact support@reviewsboost.ca with the order reference. This page does not limit non-waivable consumer rights under applicable Canadian law.

Before ordering

Ask for the exact scope, total price, start point, cancellation terms, delivery conditions and guarantee limits in writing.
