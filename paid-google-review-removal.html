How paid Google review removal works at ReviewsBoost

Send ReviewsBoost the Google reviews you want assessed. We review your case, explain the work and fees in writing, and handle the reporting and agreed follow-up once you approve.

Start with your business details and the number of reviews. If you have a review link or a short description, include it.

See ReviewsBoost pricing in CAD

What does ReviewsBoost do after you send a review?

We look at the public review and the facts you share to identify the concern and what evidence would help. For example, if a review describes another location, we’ll look at which listing the post appears on and what connects the comment to a different business. If you have several reviews, we can assess them together and identify which ones belong in the proposed work.

Include the public links for each location when they’re available. Your Google password stays private.

When does paid work start?

Once we accept the scope, we send the proposed work and fees in writing. You can check the review list and the success-fee terms before approving. The free assessment form takes no payment. When you approve the work, ReviewsBoost charges CAD $45 upfront per accepted review and CAD $95 for each review that meets the agreed removal terms.

Calculate totals for different outcomes before you decide.

What does ReviewsBoost handle once you approve?

We organize the evidence, prepare the report around the relevant Google Maps policy, and handle the reporting and follow-up included in your order. We track each review separately so you can tell which reviews have been addressed. We’ll explain any step that needs your action in your Business Profile.

Can ReviewsBoost help if you already reported the review?

Yes. Include the last status or decision with your request. That gives us a starting point for checking what was submitted and whether an appeal is available. If the case can proceed, we’ll explain what we can prepare and what we still need from you.

What updates will you receive?

We share progress as the case moves forward, including requests for more information and the outcome for each review. If only some reviews meet the agreed removal terms, the success fee applies to those reviews individually. See the fee and cancellation terms for the details.
