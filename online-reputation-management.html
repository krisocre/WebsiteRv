Reputation recovery

Build a system that lasts beyond one review

Weekly monitoring routine

  • Check new reviews and profile changes.
  • Assign every response or report to one owner.
  • Move customer-specific details to a private channel.
  • Record recurring issues by location and service line.
  • Escalate credible safety, privacy or legal concerns.

Monthly reputation review

  • Compare response coverage and response time.
  • Review rating movement alongside review volume.
  • Identify repeated praise and complaint themes.
  • Confirm profile information and public links are accurate.
  • Document the operational change chosen for the next month.

Online reputation management FAQ

What does online reputation management include?

It can include bad-review removal, profile monitoring, public response planning and a recovery process for recurring issues.

Can I send you any bad Google review?

Yes. Send us the Google Business Profile URL and the bad review. Our team will review it and explain the next step.

Should a business respond while a removal report is pending?

Often, yes. A calm response can show future customers that the business takes feedback seriously. Avoid exposing private facts or arguing the full case in public.

Does ReviewsBoost work across Canada?

Yes. The online service covers business locations in every province and territory, including smaller towns and rural communities.

What information should I send first?

Send the business name, location, Business Profile URL, review link or text and your contact details. Do not send account passwords or sensitive personal information.
