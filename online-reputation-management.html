The process

A defensible path from review to resolution

Each step produces a useful decision or record, even when the platform keeps the review online.

Capture

Record the review URL, text, reviewer details, publication date and relevant business records before anything changes.

Classify

Separate a genuine negative experience from spam, conflicts, harassment, personal information or other possible violations.

Act

Use the strongest available reporting route, preserve submission details and prepare a factual response when appropriate.

Monitor

Track the platform decision, response status, repeated themes and the next action owner and date.

Review triage

Different review problems require different responses

A low star rating alone is not proof of a policy violation. Accurate classification avoids weak reports and helps the business focus on the action it can support.

Fake engagement, conflicts of interest, threats, harassment, exposed personal information, promotional spam or content unrelated to an experience may support a report.

Reputation recovery

Build a system that lasts beyond one review

Weekly monitoring routine

  • Check new reviews and profile changes.
  • Assign every response or report to one owner.
  • Move customer-specific details to a private channel.
  • Record recurring issues by location and service line.
  • Escalate credible safety, privacy or legal concerns.

Monthly reputation review

  • Compare response coverage and response time.
  • Review rating movement alongside review volume.
  • Identify repeated praise and complaint themes.
  • Confirm profile information and public links are accurate.
  • Document the operational change chosen for the next month.

Online reputation management FAQ

What does online reputation management include?

It can include reputation assessment, review monitoring, policy-based reporting, public response planning, evidence organization and a recovery process for recurring issues.

Can every negative Google review be removed?

No. A negative opinion may remain when it reflects a genuine experience and follows policy. Google makes the final moderation decision.

Should a business respond while a removal report is pending?

Often, yes. A calm response can show future customers that the business takes feedback seriously. Avoid exposing private facts or arguing the full case in public.

Does ReviewsBoost work across Canada?

Yes. The online service covers business locations in every province and territory, including smaller towns and rural communities.

What information should I send first?

Send the business name, location, Business Profile URL, review link or text, the reason for concern and any dated evidence. Do not send account passwords or sensitive personal information.
