Facebook eligibility

Bad reviews require a specific policy reason

A real customer’s critical Recommendation that does not violate Meta’s rules usually remains eligible to stay online.

Policy signal

No genuine experience

The account appears unrelated to any customer, visitor or service interaction you can identify.

Policy signal

The content targets a person with abuse, hateful conduct, threats or repeated unwanted contact.

Questions

Facebook review removal FAQ

Can every bad Facebook review be removed?

No. Facebook makes the final decision. A review needs a credible policy violation; a negative opinion alone is not sufficient.

How much does Facebook review removal cost?

Each accepted review costs $30 CAD upfront. The $45 CAD success fee applies only if that review is removed.

Why is the success fee different by platform?

Each platform uses different rules, evidence expectations and follow-up paths. Facebook is classified as focused work, so its success fee reflects the case effort required.

What should I send first?

Send the Facebook business or employer profile URL, the review link or screenshots, the reason it may violate policy and dated supporting records. Do not send passwords.

What happens if the review stays online?

The success fee is not charged for that review. The upfront fee covers assessment and case preparation under the refund and service terms.

Can you submit a response instead?

Yes. When a review appears eligible to remain, a careful public response and internal recovery plan may be more useful than repeated reporting.
