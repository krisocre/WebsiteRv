Know what the service covers.

Our work covers assessment, case preparation and agreed reporting support. Google decides whether a review violates its policies. A low rating or a disagreement alone is not a policy violation, and we cannot guarantee deletion or a decision date.

You can report a review directly to Google for free. Our fees cover professional assistance. Read Google’s reporting guidance.
