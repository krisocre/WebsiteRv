Know before you submit

What Is—and Isn’t—Eligible

A negative rating alone is not enough. The review needs a credible policy issue that can be challenged.

Often eligible for removal

  • Fake reviews with no genuine customer experience
  • Competitor, employee, or personal conflicts of interest
  • Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal information
  • Spam, duplicate, promotional, or off-topic content

Usually not eligible

  • Honest criticism from a verified customer
  • A low star rating without a policy violation
  • Feedback you disagree with but cannot disprove
  • Reviews that are negative but remain relevant and civil
