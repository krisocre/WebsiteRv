About ReviewsBoost

Practical support for a clearer online reputation

ReviewsBoost serves Canadian businesses with Google review resources and online reputation support. Our public learning centre focuses on useful, verifiable steps owners can apply.

Independent service

ReviewsBoost is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google. Google and Google Business Profile are trademarks of Google LLC.

What you can find here

The ReviewsBoost learning centre covers authentic customer review requests, public responses, policy-based reporting, Google Business Profile fundamentals, local SEO, rating math and long-tail business questions. Guides are written for owners, location managers, marketers and customer-service teams in Canada.

Practical workflows

Checklists, scripts, examples and implementation plans that can be adapted to a real operation.

Clear boundaries

Platform controls and moderation outcomes are described without claiming access to an algorithm or guaranteed outcomes.

Primary sources

Material policy claims link to Google, Canadian government or other authoritative source pages.

Our resource principles

  • Write for a person with a real question, not a word-count target.
  • Separate published policy, general practice and our interpretation.
  • Do not invent authors, credentials, case studies or statistics.
  • Show a review date on material guides.
  • Correct meaningful errors and update changed links.
  • Protect customer privacy in examples and avoid identifying details.
  • Do not promise a ranking, review rating or moderation result.

What we are not

The learning centre is not a law firm, regulator or official Google publication. General information is not a replacement for legal, privacy, platform or industry-specific advice. A linked source may change after a guide is published; the source itself controls.

Services and outcomes

ReviewsBoost offers online reputation support, including assessment of reviews that may violate platform policy. Google makes final decisions about content on its platform. Before buying or using any service, read the current scope, price and terms presented for that service and ask questions about any claim that is important to the decision.

Corrections and questions

Email support@reviewsboost.ca with the page URL, the statement in question and a primary source where possible. We also welcome accessibility feedback and broken-link reports.
