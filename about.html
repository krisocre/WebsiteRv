About ReviewsBoost

Online reputation support for businesses across Canada

ReviewsBoost helps Canadian businesses assess harmful reviews, organize policy-based removal cases, plan public responses and build a repeatable reputation monitoring process.

Independent service

ReviewsBoost is independent from Google, Meta, Yelp, Tripadvisor, Trustpilot and Glassdoor. Each company owns its respective marks and controls moderation on its platform.

Our reputation management approach

Our service starts by classifying the problem. Content that may violate policy needs evidence and the correct reporting route. A genuine complaint needs a careful response and an operational fix. Profile errors and recurring patterns need monitoring, ownership and a documented follow-up date.

Practical workflows

Evidence checklists, response frameworks and monitoring routines that can be adapted to a real operation.

Clear boundaries

Platform controls and moderation outcomes are described without claiming access to an algorithm or guaranteed outcomes.

Primary sources

Material policy claims link to official platform, Canadian government or other authoritative source pages.

A Canadian service area, delivered online

Our paid services are available to business locations in Canada, including all ten provinces and all three territories. We work online with businesses in large metropolitan areas, smaller towns and rural communities. Prices on our service pages are in Canadian dollars (CAD).

The Canadian service coverage section identifies representative markets and the information needed for a location-specific enquiry. These are customer service areas, not claims of a physical ReviewsBoost office in each city. For a business with multiple locations, provide the individual Business Profile links.

Our educational guides and free tools remain publicly accessible. Access to a resource from another country does not mean that paid services are offered there.

Our resource principles

  • Write for a person with a real question, not a word-count target.
  • Separate published policy, general practice and our interpretation.
  • Do not invent authors, credentials, case studies or statistics.
  • Show a review date on material guides.
  • Correct meaningful errors and update changed links.
  • Protect customer privacy in examples and avoid identifying details.
  • Do not promise a ranking, review rating or moderation result.

What we are not

The learning centre is not a law firm, regulator or official publication of any review platform. General information is not a replacement for legal, privacy, platform or industry-specific advice. A linked source may change after a guide is published; the source itself controls.

Services and outcomes

ReviewsBoost offers reputation assessment, policy-based review removal assistance for Google, Facebook, Yelp, Tripadvisor, Trustpilot and Glassdoor, response planning and recovery guidance. Each platform makes final decisions about its content. Before engaging any service, read its current scope, price and terms and ask about any claim that matters to the decision.

Corrections and questions

Email support@reviewsboost.ca with the page URL, the statement in question and a primary source where possible. We also welcome accessibility feedback and broken-link reports.
