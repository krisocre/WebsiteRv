Tripadvisor eligibility

Bad reviews require a specific policy reason

Tripadvisor says disagreement alone is not enough and does not referee ordinary factual disputes between a property and a traveler.

Policy signal

No firsthand travel experience

The writer did not stay, dine, book, visit or otherwise have a qualifying direct experience.

Policy signal

Biased or affiliated content

The writer appears connected to the property, a competitor, an employee or another conflicted party.

Policy signal

Wrong property or irrelevant claims

The review belongs elsewhere or focuses on matters unrelated to the listed travel experience.

Policy signal

Fraud, threats or private details

The content shows manipulation, abusive material, prohibited personal data or coordinated activity.

A low score is not proof of a violation. We screen the policy basis before accepting a case and do not promise that criticism will be removed.

Questions

Tripadvisor review removal FAQ

Can every bad Tripadvisor review be removed?

No. Tripadvisor makes the final decision. A review needs a credible policy violation; a negative opinion alone is not sufficient.

How much does Tripadvisor review removal cost?

Each accepted review costs $30 CAD upfront. The $65 CAD success fee applies only if that review is removed.

Why is the success fee different by platform?

Each platform uses different rules, evidence expectations and follow-up paths. Tripadvisor is classified as specialist work, so its success fee reflects the case effort required.

What should I send first?

Send the Tripadvisor business or employer profile URL, the review link or screenshots, the reason it may violate policy and dated supporting records. Do not send passwords.

What happens if the review stays online?

The success fee is not charged for that review. The upfront fee covers assessment and case preparation under the refund and service terms.

Can you submit a response instead?

Yes. When a review appears eligible to remain, a careful public response and internal recovery plan may be more useful than repeated reporting.
