Before you send an appeal

      
Use Google’s Reviews Management Tool to check eligibility. Google offers a one-time appeal for eligible reviews it declines to remove. Compare the exact wording with Google Maps content policies and include evidence that supports your concern.

      
The builder uses your selected stage and policy to provide a blank outline. Fill in the placeholders in your own copy and submit through Google’s official reporting process. For a demand tied to removing reviews, use the merchant extortion route.
